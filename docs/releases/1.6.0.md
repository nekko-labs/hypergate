# The gate is made of light again

The Hypergate mark drops its hard outline and starts turning, everywhere it appears.

### Features

- **The mark glows instead of being drawn.** The ring's edges now fade into the space around them, the way the gate on hypergate.app does, rather than ending on a stroke — in the tray icon, the app window icon, the manager's top bar and the favicon.
- **The startup window and the top bar mark rotate slowly.** Light drifts around the gate while the daemon comes up, and the manager's mark keeps turning while the app is open.

### Bug fixes

- **The grey line across the top of the icon is gone.** A pale highlight sat over the ring and read as a scratch at icon sizes; the brand gradient carries the highlight now.
- **A failed start no longer spins forever.** When Hypergate cannot bring the daemon up, the startup window's gate stops instead of implying it is still waiting.

### Performance improvements

- **The new motion costs nothing to speak of.** It runs entirely on the GPU compositor — no repaints, no 3D context, and no animation at all for anyone whose system asks for reduced motion.
